# Stack Rush - Positioning Strategy

## Unique Value Proposition

**Stack Rush is the satisfying stacking game that respects your time.**

While other stacking games bombard you with ads every few seconds, Stack Rush delivers a polished, premium experience with:
- Smooth 60fps gameplay
- Satisfying haptic feedback
- 13 beautiful themes to unlock
- A fair $2.99 one-time upgrade (no subscriptions)

## Pricing Position

### Price: $2.99 (One-Time Premium)

**Why This Price?**
- Below "coffee price" psychological threshold
- Higher conversion than $4.99+
- Lifetime value comparable to subscription after 1 month
- Signals quality without exclusivity

**Value Framing**
- "Less than a coffee, hours of entertainment"
- "Pay once, play forever - no subscriptions"
- "All themes, no ads, 2x coins"

## Success Indicators

### Positioning Validation
- Users describe game as "satisfying" in reviews
- Premium conversion > 2%
- Organic mentions compare favorably to Stack
- "No ads" mentioned positively in reviews

### Repositioning Triggers
- If conversion < 1%: Test lower price point
- If retention low: Add more progression
- If downloads low: Broaden positioning to "casual puzzle"
