# Stack Rush - Market Research

## Market Overview

### Casual Mobile Gaming Market
- **Global Market Size (2025)**: $98.7 billion
- **Annual Growth Rate**: 8.5% CAGR
- **Mobile Share of Gaming**: 52% of total gaming revenue
- **Hyper-Casual Games**: Fastest growing segment at 12% YoY

### Key Statistics
- Average mobile gamer plays 3.5 games weekly
- Session length for casual games: 3-7 minutes
- 78% of mobile gamers prefer free-to-play with optional purchases
- Stacking/puzzle games consistently rank in top 100 grossing

## Target Audience

### Primary Demographics
- **Age**: 18-45
- **Gender**: Slightly female-skewed (52%)
- **Device**: Mid-to-high-end smartphones
- **Play Context**: Commuting, waiting rooms, breaks

### Psychographics
- Seeks quick entertainment during short breaks
- Enjoys satisfying, tactile gameplay
- Motivated by personal bests and progression
- Willing to pay for ad-free experience
- Values visual polish and smooth animations

### User Personas

**"The Commuter" (Sarah, 28)**
- Plays during 30-min train commute
- Wants quick games, no commitment
- Hates interruptive ads
- Likely to pay $2.99 to remove ads

**"The Score Chaser" (Mike, 35)**
- Competitive, wants to beat personal bests
- Plays in 5-minute bursts
- Motivated by progression systems
- Will grind for unlockables

**"The Casual Parent" (Lisa, 42)**
- Plays while kids do homework
- Values simple, non-violent games
- Appreciates clean design
- Downloads games kids recommend

## Market Trends

### Rising Trends
1. **Premium Casual**: Players willing to pay for quality ad-free experiences
2. **One-Time Purchases**: Subscription fatigue driving preference for one-time IAP
3. **Haptic Feedback**: Tactile responses becoming expected feature
4. **Minimalist Design**: Clean UI preferred over cluttered screens
5. **Offline Play**: Users want games that work without internet

### Declining Trends
1. Banner ads (user rejection high)
2. Energy systems (friction causes churn)
3. Mandatory accounts (privacy concerns)
4. Social requirements (not all users want this)

## Revenue Model Analysis

### Freemium with One-Time Premium
- **Conversion Rate**: 2-5% industry average
- **ARPU**: $0.15-0.50 (with ads), $0.50-2.00 (premium conversion)
- **LTV**: Higher than subscription for casual games

### Why $2.99 One-Time Purchase
- Price point below impulse threshold
- Higher conversion than $4.99+
- Perceived value: "coffee price" framing
- No subscription management overhead
- Better reviews (users don't feel nickel-and-dimed)

## Distribution Strategy

### App Store Optimization (ASO)
- Target keywords with 30-50 difficulty score
- Focus on "stacking game", "block game", "casual puzzle"
- Localize for top 10 markets

### Organic Growth Vectors
1. Word of mouth (satisfying gameplay = shares)
2. App Store featuring (target Editorial)
3. Social media clips (satisfying stacking moments)
4. Reddit communities (r/iosgaming, r/AndroidGaming)
